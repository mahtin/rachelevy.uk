At 95 years old I’ve made it into space. Well, kinda.

This space selfie picture was taken while a satellite flew over London on Monday June 30'th, 2025.
It’s a satellite built explicitly to display uploaded pictures on a smartphone, then snap a selfie, and then send it back to earth as a new selfie picture.
The satellite is called [SAT GUS](https://www.spaceselfie.com) and it was launched by SpaceX, where my grandson works.

This was all setup by my son Martin.
He described the picture:

>	If you’re wondering - the view is of Amsterdam on the left side and the French coast and English Channel above the selfie picture.
>	London is just below the satellite and can’t be seen.
>	The satellite looking southwards.
>	The picture was taken on Monday around 12:45 (just around lunchtime) from 300 miles up in space! 
>
>	Look at #spaceselfie on social media for more.

I never actually went to space, just this photo!
